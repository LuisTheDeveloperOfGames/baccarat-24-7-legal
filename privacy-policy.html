Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2, 2026

Simple Summary: This app does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Everything stays on your device.

1. What We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect any personal information, including:

  • No names or email addresses
  • No location data
  • No payment information
  • No photos or camera access
  • No contacts or phone data
  • No usage analytics or tracking

2. How Your Game Data is Stored

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device using local storage. This includes:

  • Your Baccarat Bucks balance
  • Game history
  • Age verification status
  • Disclaimer acceptance

This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, all this data is deleted with it.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. The app includes an age gate that prevents access for users under 18.

5. Virtual Currency

Important: All "Baccarat Bucks" in this app are virtual and have no real-world value. They cannot be:

  • Converted to real money
  • Withdrawn or cashed out
  • Transferred to other users
  • Used to purchase anything outside the app

6. Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there is nothing to secure or protect on our end. Your local game data is protected by your device's security features.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
